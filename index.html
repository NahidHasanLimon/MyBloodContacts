Official Blood Contacts App

Trusted donor contact management with optional Google Drive backup.

Blood Contacts helps users organize donor contacts, track urgent blood needs, and quickly call or message matching donors. Data stays local by default, with optional Google Drive sync when you choose.

About Data Access and Google Sign-In

Blood Contacts is the official website for the Blood Contacts app. The app uses Google Sign-In only when you choose optional Google Drive backup and sync.

  • Google OAuth scope used: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file
  • Purpose: create, update, and restore Blood Contacts backup files in your Drive account
  • Android permissions used: Contacts read/write only for donor contact features
  • No broad photo-library permission is requested by the app
